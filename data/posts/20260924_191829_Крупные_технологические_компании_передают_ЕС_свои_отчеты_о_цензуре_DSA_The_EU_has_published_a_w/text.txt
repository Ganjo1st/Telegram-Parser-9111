**Крупные технологические компании передают ЕС свои отчеты о цензуре DSA**

The EU has published a wide range of reports detailing what social media companies like TikTok and Meta are doing to comply with its Digital Services Act (DSA). The reports detail the extreme censorship needed to stay on Brussels’ good side. Under the European Commission’s“voluntary Code of Conduct on Disinformation,”the largest online platforms operating in the EU are required to submit biannual reports explaining their efforts to combat so-назвал“disinformation.”Although adherence to the code is described as“voluntary,”those who do not comply are subject to fines under the DSA, which came into force in 2023. The commission published reports by Meta, TikTok, Google, Twitch, and more than two dozen other platforms on Wednesday. Each report spans hundreds of pages, and details the speech restrictions and censorship policies put in place at the behest of the EU.
