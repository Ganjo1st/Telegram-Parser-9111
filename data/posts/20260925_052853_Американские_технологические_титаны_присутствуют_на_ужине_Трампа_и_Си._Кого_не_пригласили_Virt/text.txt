**Американские технологические титаны присутствуют на ужине Трампа и Си. Кого не пригласили?**

Virtually all of America’s most powerful технологии executives joined US президент Donald Trump and китайский leader Xi Jinping for a lavish White House state dinner on Thursday, as искусственного интеллекта emerged as a major theme of the китайский leader’s visit. The black-tie dinner in the White House East Room brought together more than 130 guests, including Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and президент Greg Brockman, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella and Google CEO Sundar Pichai. Apple chairman Tim Cook, AMD chief Lisa Su, Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon, Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra, Zoom CEO Eric Yuan and Dell Technologies chief Michael Dell were also among those invited.
