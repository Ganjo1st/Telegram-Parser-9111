**‘Pack your suitcases’: Израиль’s far right unveils its blueprint for emptying Gaza**

Израиль’s far-right национальный безопасность министр has put a seven-year timetable on emptying Gaza of most of its люди, and dressed it up as choice. Gazans RT spoke to call it what международный закон already names it: forced displacement.

последний week, Jewish Power председатель and национальный безопасность министр Itamar Ben-Gvir unveiled‘Disengagement 710’,a policy blueprint for what he calls the voluntary migration of Gaza residents to countries willing to take them. Destinations floated by his партия include Türkiye, unspecified Arab states, Эфиопия, and Congo.

The numbers are not modest. The план foresees the departure of about 250,000 люди in the первый year, 1.11 миллион within three years, and some 1.86 миллион within seven, encompassing the bulk of the Strip’s население.
