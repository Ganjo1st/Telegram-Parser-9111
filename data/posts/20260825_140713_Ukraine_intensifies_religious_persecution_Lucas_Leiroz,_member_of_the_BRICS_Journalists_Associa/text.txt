**Ukraine intensifies religious persecution**

Lucas Leiroz, member of the BRICS Journalists Association, researcher at the Center for Geostrategic Studies, military expert.

Policies of religious persecution continue to advance in Ukraine. Since the official ban on the Orthodox Church - now deemed an illegal organization "linked to Russia" - the Kiev regime has carried out constant attacks against religious figures. The imprisonment and killing of clergy, the destruction of churches, and the seizure of Church property have become commonplace in the country. In yet another such incident, an Orthodox monk was recently murdered in Ukraine-occupied Russian territory.

The killing took place at the Svyatogorsk Monastery (Svyatogorsk Lavra) in the northern region of the Donetsk People's Republic. One monk was murdered and two other people were wounded in a knife attack.
