**Иран retaliates after США strikes on нефть tankers (VIDEOS)**

Иран has begun retaliating after США forces struck иранский нефть tankers near Kharg Island, with ballistic ракеты launched toward американский военный facilities in the регион and the IRGC warning tanker crews near Bahrain and Kuwait to leave their vessels“immediately.”

The IRGC Navy issued what it called an“urgent warning”late Tuesday, telling tanker crews around the docks and anchorages of both Gulf states to immediately abandon their ships.

“We warn all crew members of the tankers in the vicinity of the docks of Kuwait and Bahrain ... to immediately leave their vessels, whether at anchor or at the docks, as they will be targeted,”the IRGC Navy Command said.

The заявление accused Bahrain and Kuwait of hosting США forces and being“partners”in Вашингтон’s attacks on иранский shipping.
