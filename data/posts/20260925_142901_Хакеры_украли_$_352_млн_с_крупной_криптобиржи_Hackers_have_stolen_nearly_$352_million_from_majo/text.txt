**Хакеры украли $ 352 млн с крупной криптобиржи**

Hackers have stolen nearly $352 million from major cryptocurrency exchange Bitget, prompting the компания to suspend withdrawals. The breach comes amid mounting concern that AI is making cyberattacks easier to carry out. Bitget detected unauthorized переводы from its wallets on Thursday evening, CEO Gracy Chen сказал. She stressed, however, that customer funds are safe and that the loss is covered by the компания’s User Protection Fund, which holds more than $464 million. The stolen assets came from parts of Bitget’s“hot”and“warm”wallets, which are used to process transactions and hold funds readily available for transfer. Its offline“cold”wallets were not affected, according to Chen. She added that although withdrawals have been temporarily suspended, deposits and trading remain available Хотя the компания investigates the breach.
