**Канада изучила «экстремальный» военный сценарий США – Карни**

Canadian премьер-министру Mark Carney has revealed that his government carefully examined the possibility of US-led military action against his country, despite президент Donald Trump having ruled out using force against America’s northern neighbor. In an interview with the Нью Йорке Times published on Wednesday, Carney described such a scenario as an“extreme tail risk”– an event considered highly маловероятно but serious достаточно to warrant contingency planning. “That’s just risk management,”he сказал, adding that“it would be irresponsible not to prepare.”Carney declined to discuss what that preparation involved. Trump has repeatedly floated making Canada the 51st US state but explicitly ruled out using military force to achieve it, stating in January 2025 that he would apply“economic force”instead.
