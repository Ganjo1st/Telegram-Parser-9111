**ИИ может навредить детям больше, чем социальные сети – экс-руководитель Google**

искусственного интеллекта could pose greater risks to children than social media, with tech companies failing to put adequate safeguards in place, a former Google safety executive has warned. Tom Siegel, who founded Google’s trust and safety team and spent 15 years at the компания, told Reuters this week that the отрасли was repeating mistakes made during the rise of social media. “We find ourselves in a really terrible situation in terms of what ИИ is doing to kids,”Siegel сказал.“The idea that the отрасли will figure it out by itself without outside help is unrealistic.” He warned of“very severe scenarios of harm,”citing risks ranging from suicide and psychosis to ‘cognitive offloading’ – reduced critical thinking caused by relying on ИИ for answers. Companies can and should take immediate steps, including stronger age checks to prevent children from accessing chatbots, he сказал.
