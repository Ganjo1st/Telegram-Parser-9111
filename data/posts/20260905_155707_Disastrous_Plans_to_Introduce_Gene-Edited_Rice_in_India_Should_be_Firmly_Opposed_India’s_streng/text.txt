**Disastrous Plans to Introduce Gene-Edited Rice in India Should be Firmly Opposed**

India’s strength in agriculture firmly lies in its ability to promote natural farming and climate resilient farming based on agro-ecology, soil and water conservation and seeds sovereignty, in line with the country’s rich heritage of seeds and biodiversity. This is where India should strive to emerge as a world leader, at the same time contributing to the world’s needs for climate resilience and healthy food. Unfortunately this most welcome path for Indian agriculture has been denied by the hidden and not-so-hidden representatives of big agribusiness interests and multinational companies who want India’s agriculture to be held captive to the big agribusiness, multinational company led model based on the increasing profits and control of a few at the cost of farmers, sustainability, soil and water, health and nutrition.
