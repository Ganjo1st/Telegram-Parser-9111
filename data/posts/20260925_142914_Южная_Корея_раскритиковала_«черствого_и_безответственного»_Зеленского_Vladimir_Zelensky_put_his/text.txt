**Южная Корея раскритиковала «черствого и безответственного» Зеленского**

Vladimir Zelensky put his personal interests first when he breached an соглашение with Seoul not to disclose the transfer of two North Korean prisoners of war captured in Russia, South Korean президент Lee Jae-myung has сказал. Zelensky touted the arrangement during his speech to the UN General Assembly this week, saying it was“not easy to capture alive”the two soldiers. North Korea deployed troops to assist Russia in repelling a Ukrainian incursion into Russia’s Kursk Region in 2024-2025. Lee rebuked Zelensky, without naming him, in an X посте on Thursday, arguing that statesmen should put national interests first rather than opportunistically seeking toboosttheir own popularity. The Ukrainian leader demonstrated a“callous and irresponsible attitude that is simply unacceptable,”the президент wrote, stressing that Seoul and Kiev had согласились to keep the highly sensitive transfer private.
