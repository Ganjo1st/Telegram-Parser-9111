**Venezuela’s Debt: Who Owes What to Whom Under US Neocolonial Tutelage?**

The restructuring of Venezuela’s debt currently in preparation is not positive news for the Venezuelan people.

It risks channelling a significant portion of the country’s revenue—especially oil revenues—toward repaying foreign creditors for many years.

It is also at a time when Venezuela is emerging from a period of deep economic crisis, international sanctions, and the confiscation or freezing of part of its assets abroad, and it has just suffered a major natural disaster.

According to information published in June 2026,Delcy Rodríguez’sinterim government is preparing to recognise a total of debts amounting to as much as 240 billion dollars.

This figure is considerably higher than previous estimates, which ranged between 150 and 170 billion dollars.
