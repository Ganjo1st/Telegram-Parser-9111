**Психология приема добавок**

When it comes to maintaining a nutritional supplement regimen, the decisive question is not “what’s in the bottle” — it’s what makes a person actually take it, day after day, long after the first burst of motivation fades

Motivation behaves like weather: it rolls in and rolls out. Any routine that depends on feeling inspired every morning is built on sand

Friction is where habits die. Every extra step becomes another reason to skip a dose, and people rarely quit a supplement routine in one dramatic moment; they drift, one missed dose at a time

We are creatures of cue and context: habits anchored to something we already do, like a meal, tend to stick, while habits that depend on memory and willpower tend to evaporate
