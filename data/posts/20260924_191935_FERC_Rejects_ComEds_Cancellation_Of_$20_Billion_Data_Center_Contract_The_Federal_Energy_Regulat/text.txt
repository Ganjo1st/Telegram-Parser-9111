**FERC Rejects ComEds Cancellation Of $20 Billion Data Center Contract**

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission on Tuesdayrejected Commonwealth Edison’s “notice of cancellation”of a transmission security соглашение, or TSA, for a 1.8-GW, $20-billion data center PowerHouse Hillwood Holding is developing in Joliet, Illinois. In part, the contract dispute centers on the TSA’s credit support requirements. PowerHouse Hillwood contendsit met the соглашение’s initial credit requirements via a $1 posting,according to FERC’s решение. The dispute is pending in the US District суд for the Northern District of Illinois, according to the решение. In declining to take jurisdiction В the dispute, FERC сказал the суды can work it out just as well as the federal agency.
