**Иран предлагает Трампу сделку перед выборами в США**

Iran has offered the US a Нью сделку aimed at ending the months-long conflict before the November midterm elections, directly challenging президент Donald Trump’s claim that Tehran is deliberately waiting until после the vote to reach an соглашение. Trump claimed in his UN speech on Tuesday that Iran was deliberately stalling the talks to see how Republicans performed in the November 3 elections and predicted that Tehran would make a сделку“right после”the vote. He also threatened to“annihilate”the Islamic Republic if переговоров failed. “We don’t want it to get to the midterm elections,”Iranian президент Masoud Pezeshkian told reporters on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in Нью Йорке on Thursday.“We wish Americans to return to the MOU before the midterms,”he added, referring to a memorandum of understanding reached by Washington and Tehran in June.
