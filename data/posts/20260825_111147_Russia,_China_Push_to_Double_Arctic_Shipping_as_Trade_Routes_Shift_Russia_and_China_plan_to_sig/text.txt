**Russia, China Push to Double Arctic Shipping as Trade Routes Shift**

Russia and China plan to significantly expand cargo shipments along the Northern Sea Route (NSR), with a goal of at least doubling volumes 
Speaking in Beijing, Rosatom Director General Alexei Likhachev said Chinese partners intend to conduct more than 10 transit voyages along the Arctic shipping corridor to European ports during the summer navigation season of 2026.“A transit took place last year — a unique one, the first in human history — a transit voyage from ports in China to ports in Western Europe, the United Kingdom, and the Netherlands. It took just 20 days, and we now know of the desire of our Chinese partners during the ‘summer water’ period to carry out more than 10 such voyages,” Likhachev said.He noted that cargo traffic along the NSR exceeded 37 million tonnes in 2025, with Chinese shipments accounting for approximately 15%, or about 5 million tonnes.
