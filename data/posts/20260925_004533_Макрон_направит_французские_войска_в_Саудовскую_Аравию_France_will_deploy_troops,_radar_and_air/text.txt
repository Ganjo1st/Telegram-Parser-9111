**Макрон направит французские войска в Саудовскую Аравию**

France will deploy troops, radar and air-defense systems to Saudi Arabia to protect a key Red Sea oil hub as Riyadh struggles to contain escalating Houthi missile and drone attacks, президент Emmanuel Macron has announced. Macron claimed that French forces would not join the fighting but would merely protect the strategic energy facility under an соглашение finalized with Riyadh. The announcement came часов после the Saudi-led coalition сказал it had intercepted six ballistic missiles heading toward Taif, which hosts a key Saudi air base, and the strategic port city of Yanbu. “We are going to send military resources, that is to говорят, soldiers, radar systems and defense systems, to protect the site of Yanbu,”Macron told TF1 and France 2 on Thursday. Macron сказал the Yanbu hub, which handles several million barrels of oil, had been“very badly damaged”in previous attacks.
