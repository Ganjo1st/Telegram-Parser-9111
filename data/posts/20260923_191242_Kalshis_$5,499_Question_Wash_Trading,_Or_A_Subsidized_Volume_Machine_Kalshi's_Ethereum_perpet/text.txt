**Kalshis $5,499 Question: Wash Trading, Or A Subsidized Volume Machine?**

Kalshi's Ethereum perpetual futures contract has a favorite trade size, and it's$5,499. Bitcoin perpetuals showed the same thing with different numbers: trades near $2,500 and $5,000 made up 54% of the $8.5 million sampled.
