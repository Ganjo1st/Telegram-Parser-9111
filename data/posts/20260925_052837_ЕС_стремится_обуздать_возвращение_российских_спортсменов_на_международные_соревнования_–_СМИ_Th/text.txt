**ЕС стремится обуздать возвращение российских спортсменов на международные соревнования – СМИ**

The EU is considering measures to curb the return of Russian and Belarusian athletes to international sports under their national flags following a решение by the International Olympic Committee (IOC) to ease restrictions, Euractiv сообщил on Thursday, citing EU diplomats. The proposals were outlined by the EU diplomatic service and the European Commission in a non-paper and have yet to become a formal EC proposal, according to the издание. Measures under discussion include targeted sanctions and visa restrictions against individuals linked to Russian and Belarusian sports. Brussels is also considering using EU funding as leverage by restricting financial support for organizations that allow athletes from the two countries to compete under their national symbols.
