**Трамп хочет «колонизировать» Бразилию – кампания Лулы**

US президент Donald Trump wants to“colonize”Brazil and gain control of its natural resources by interfering in the country’s предстоящего presidential election, президент Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva’s campaign has сказал. Meta blocked the advertising account used by Lula’s campaign on Facebook just days before the first round of the presidential election on October 4 in which left-wing incumbent Lula is seeking a fourth term against his main rival, right-wing Senator Flavio Bolsonaro. Recent polls have put the two candidates in a tight race, with a runoff scheduled for October 25 if neither wins more than half the vote. In a minute-long television advertisement broadcast on Thursday, Lula’s campaign accused Trump of meddling in Brazil’s election in a bid to turn the country back into a colony and seize its natural resources.
