**Белый дом восстановил доступ к запрещенным СМИ после решения судей**

Update (1230ET):Following US District Judge Timothy J. Kelly's earlier решение, the Trump administration сказал in a legal filing thatit has restored access for now to three media outlets the президент had banned from White House grounds last week. Reporters from CNN and MS NOW started returning to the White House grounds после a federal суд had ordered a suspension of the ban. The networks made announcements during their broadcasts on Thursday. As Melanie Sun сообщил earlier for The Epoch Times, afederal judge has ordered президент Donald Trump to temporarily restore White House access for CNN, MS NOW, and Politico Хотя they challenge the revocation of their press passes in суд. Federal Judge Timothy Kelly of the U.S. District суд for the District of Columbiaissueda temporary restraining order against the White House ban, reinstating hard pass access for CNN, MS NOW, and Politico in the early часов of Sept.
