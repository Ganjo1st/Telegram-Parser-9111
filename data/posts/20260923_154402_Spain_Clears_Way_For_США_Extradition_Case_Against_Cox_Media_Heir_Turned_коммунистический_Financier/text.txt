**Spain Clears Way For США Extradition Case Against Cox Media Heir Turned коммунистический Financier**

TheSpanish правительство has begun a processthat could soon lead to the США extradition of Jim "Fergie" Chambers, the коммунистический centimillionaire and heir to the massive Cox media fortune.

Bloombergreported Tuesday that Chambers moved a step closer to extradition to the США after Spain's Cabinet allowed the case to proceed to суд.

Chambers has been detained since hisJuly 10 arrest in Ibizaand is wanted by the США on money laundering, riot and riot conspiracy charges linked to 2023 pro-палестинский demonstrations and transfers to a компания in Tunisia, where he previously lived.

правительство spokeswoman Elma Saiz confirmed to the outlet that Spanish courts would now review the request. If judges approve extradition, the final решение returns to премьер-министр Pedro Sánchez's Cabinet.
