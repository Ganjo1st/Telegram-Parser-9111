**Пропалестинские протестующие арестованы за пределами ООН во время речи Нетаньяху (ВИДЕО)**

Dozens of pro-Palestinian protesters have been arrested near UN headquarters in Manhattan on Thursday as Israeli премьер-министру Benjamin Netanyahu addressed the General Assembly, according to RT’s Caleb Maupin, reporting from the scene. Demonstrators blocked an intersection several blocks from the UN building in a planned act of civil disobedience timed to coincide with Netanyahu’s speech, Maupin сказал. RT footage showed protesters holding признаки reading“stop settler terror”and“stop arming Israel.”Demonstrators also chanted“Netanyahu you can’t hide, we charge you with genocide”and“One, two, three, four, Gaza’s what we’re fighting for.” “The protesters are expecting to be arrested. This is a planned act of civil disobedience timed with the Israeli премьер-министру’s предстоящего speech,”Maupin сказал. Another video showed задержан protesters being loaded into an NYPD van.
