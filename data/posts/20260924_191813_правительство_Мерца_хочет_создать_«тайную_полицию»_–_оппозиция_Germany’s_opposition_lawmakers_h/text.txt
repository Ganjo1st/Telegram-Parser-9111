**правительство Мерца хочет создать «тайную полицию» – оппозиция**

Germany’s opposition lawmakers have accused Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s government of seeking to создать a“secret police”by dramatically expanding the powers of the country’s интеллекта agencies. The accusations came during the firstparliamentary debateon the reform on Thursday, with lawmakers from the Alternative for Germany (AfD), the Left and the Greens raising concerns В surveillance and fundamental rights. The government argues that the changes are needed to counter growing security угрозы. Under a reform approved by the кабинет last month, the BND foreign интеллекта agency and the BfV domestic security service would gain broader operational powers, allowing them to move beyond primarily gathering and analyzing information. Among the proposed changes, the BND could sabotage foreign companies, penetrate foreign IT systems and disable weapons deemed a threat to Germany.
