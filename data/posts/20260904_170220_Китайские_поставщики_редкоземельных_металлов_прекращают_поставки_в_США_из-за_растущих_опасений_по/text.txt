AReutersreport on Friday morning revealed that someChinese rare-earth suppliers are refusing to ship material to US customers, citing fear of retaliation from Beijing.

A handful of Chinese suppliers haverefused to ship rare earths to U.S. companies since early Augustwhen China imposed sanctions on the Responsible Business Alliance (RBA), a U.S. supply chain monitor, a separate source with direct knowledge of the situation said.
