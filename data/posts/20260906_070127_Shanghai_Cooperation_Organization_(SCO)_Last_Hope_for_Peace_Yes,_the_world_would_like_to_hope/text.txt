**Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO): Last Hope for Peace?**

Yes, the world would like to hope that there is still expectation for peace and that this hope for peace may emanate from this little heard-about in the West – the ten-member Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), an association of countries, some of whom are also part of the BRICS – that are committed to follow the same politically peaceful policies. SCO is not a military organization nor is it an economic community.

SCO is a structure that designs long-term strategy for peaceful cooperation around the world, including turning the military into a force for peace. As strange as this may sound, it is possible, and indeed there are high-ranking military officers in the West and the East – many (but not all) of them retired – who have seen the light, thanks to their experience and thanks to their deeper understanding of what peaceful co-existence really means, especially in terms of human wealth and wellbeing.
